    Frequently asked questions

    The six things riders and renters ask us most.

    Do I get a helmet?
    Yes. Every rider gets one, and the captain carries it with the scooter. Helmets are wiped down and the liner is cleaned between rides.
    How is the ride fare calculated?
    ₹20 base plus ₹6 per kilometre on the route the app plans, and nothing else. No surge, no night or waiting charge. The fare is shown before you confirm and you pay after the ride by UPI or cash.
    Can I schedule a ride?
    Yes, up to 7 days ahead. Pick a time when you book. A captain is assigned about 10 minutes before pickup, and you get their name, photo and number plate then.
    What documents do I need to rent a car?
    A valid LMV driving licence and one government photo ID (Aadhaar, passport or voter card). Drivers must be 21 or older. Bring the originals; we photograph them at pickup and hand them straight back.
    Is fuel or charging included?
    Cars go out with a full tank or a full charge and come back the same way. Return with less and we top up at pump price with no handling fee. If you rent an electric car, you can plug in at the Kinetix hub any time during the rental.
    Can I cancel?
    Rides: free any time before the captain arrives at your pickup. Cars: free up to 24 hours before pickup from your booking link; inside 24 hours we keep one hour’s rent (hourly) or one day’s rent (daily). The deposit is always returned in full.
