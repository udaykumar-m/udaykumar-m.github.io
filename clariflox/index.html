About Us

Who are we?

ClariFlox is a leading provider of water and process-treatment solutions, headquartered in Duvvada, Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh. With over 50 years of combined experience, our team brings deep technical expertise, industry insight, and a strong commitment to customer success.

Founded in Andhra Pradesh, ClariFlox is guided by integrity, quality, and technical innovation. Our experienced professionals understand the operational and regulatory challenges industries face in water and wastewater management, enabling us to deliver practical, reliable solutions.

We are a team of specialists in industrial water treatment, offering practical expertise in effluent / wastewater treatment, raw water clarification, reverse osmosis plants and cooling-water programmes. We supply high-performance chemicals such as flocculants, coagulants, antiscalants, corrosion inhibitors, dispersants, and defoamers, supported by technical guidance for reliable plant operation from multinational principles.
